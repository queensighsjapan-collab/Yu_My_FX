HOW TO USE — Click steps to program the pattern, tap a voice pad to audition it and select it for REVERB SEND. TUNE works on SNARE, RIM, TOMS and CONGAS; LEVEL sets each voice in the mix. REVERB adds space: MIX is the wet level, SIZE is the room length, DAMPING darkens the tail. SEND is per voice — kick stays almost dry by default, while SNARE, CLAP and cymbals send more. OPEN and CLOSED HAT choke each other, and TOMS share their voice circuit with CONGAS, just like the hardware era. Load your own WAV into any voice with its LOAD WAV tag. Patterns live in the 8 slots above; EXPORT writes them to a JSON file you can IMPORT again later.
